About Me

I love creative writing, creating stories and building-worlds that are full of lores that actually feels alive. I started writing short stories and full on series in 2018 in Webnovel website and I also worked for the otaku centered website "Anime Corner".

I also love games, because I believe that interactive worlds are one of the best and most beautiful form of story-telling. My passion for gaming is the fuel that fires my passion to create those said worlds. In addition, I also do game reviews on the website "Backloggd" because I feel like sharing my game experience and adding additional informations to the game is part of why I love the gaming community, the developers gives you the story in front of you, while you interact with the said world. I just love talking about how the game and story did for me throughout my whole walkthrough. Gaming, Story-Writing, and even Game Designing will always be a passion of mine that I will never quit upon.
